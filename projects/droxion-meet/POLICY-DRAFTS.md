# Droxion Meet policy drafts for legal review

These private QA drafts are incomplete and are not published user agreements.

## Privacy draft

The private QA application processes credentials, QA profile fields, likes and passes, messages, activity proposals, reports and moderator actions. Use synthetic information only. Public-facing profile views in QA show a display name, bio, interests, and intention. QA uses a designated community value internally and does not request exact coordinates or addresses. Only mutual matches may open member conversations. Authorized moderators can see submitted reports and copied message evidence. The fictional demo uses local rules and its chat state clears on page reload.

A QA member can delete their account in Settings. This removes the profile and associated member activity, messages, proposals and reports from the active database. Before launch, counsel and operations must define evidence retention, backup expiry, account recovery, disclosure obligations, rights requests, age-assurance data handling, and contact information for the service operator.

## Terms draft

The current application is reserved for assigned QA testers. Do not provide real personal information, impersonate other people, harass anyone, solicit payment, or use the app to meet real users during QA. Fictional demo bots are labeled and cannot attend activities. Activity proposals do not reserve a venue. QA has no paid subscription or real payment collection. Operators may suspend or remove access to protect the test environment.

Before launch, counsel must review eligibility, acceptable use, reporting and appeal procedures, operator identity, jurisdiction, consumer disclosures, subscription terms, cancellation, and renewal notices.
