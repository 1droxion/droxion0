Frequently asked questions

Does it find exactly the best five moments?

It picks five potential highlights using audio and motion, but it cannot understand gameplay context. Always review the clips before posting.

Does caption ON transcribe speech automatically?

No. You enter a short caption line that gets applied to each export. Automatic transcription is not included in this version.

Will this make me money?

Clips can help you publish more content, but audience growth and earnings are never guaranteed. Platform eligibility, content quality, and audience interest matter.

Can I paste any YouTube or Twitch link?

No. This local version supports uploaded files or direct public HTTPS MP4 links from footage you have permission to process.
